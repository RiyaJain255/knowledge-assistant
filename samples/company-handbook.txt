ACME Corp Employee Handbook (sample document)

1. Working hours
Core working hours are 10:00 to 16:00 local time, Monday to Friday. Outside core hours employees may
arrange their schedule with their manager. The standard working week is 40 hours.

2. Annual leave
Full-time employees receive 24 days of paid annual leave per calendar year, in addition to public
holidays. Up to 5 unused days can be carried over into the next year; carried-over days expire on
31 March. Leave requests should be submitted at least two weeks in advance through the HR portal.

3. Sick leave
Employees receive 10 paid sick days per year. A medical certificate is required for absences longer
than three consecutive days. Please notify your manager before 10:00 on the first day of absence.

4. Remote work
Employees may work remotely up to three days per week. Fully remote arrangements require written
approval from a department head and are reviewed every six months. Remote employees receive a
one-time home-office allowance of 500 USD.

5. Expense reimbursement
Business expenses must be submitted within 30 days with receipts attached. Meals during business
travel are reimbursed up to 60 USD per day. Flights over 6 hours may be booked in premium economy.
Expenses above 1,000 USD need prior approval from a director.

6. Security
All laptops use full-disk encryption. Passwords must be at least 14 characters and rotated only when
a compromise is suspected. Report lost devices or phishing emails to security@acme.example within one
hour of discovery.

7. Parental leave
Primary caregivers receive 20 weeks of fully paid parental leave. Secondary caregivers receive 8 weeks.
Leave can be taken in one block or split into two blocks within the first 12 months after birth or
adoption.
